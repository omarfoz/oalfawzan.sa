Summary

Cloud Technical Lead with 8+ years of experience helping customers move to the cloud and get the most value from it. I work across pre-sales, solution design, delivery, and cloud product management, from writing proposals and running workshops to executing migrations and managing cloud products and services.

My experience covers cloud architecture, adoption, governance, security, and cross-functional collaboration across infrastructure, applications, databases, networking, security, and operations teams. I focus on aligning cloud solutions with business requirements while ensuring scalability, security, operational efficiency, and long-term value.

I also have hands-on experience with containers, AI, automation, and cloud infrastructure, along with strong knowledge of CCoE principles, cloud governance frameworks, operating models, standards, and enterprise cloud adoption.

Biography

2014 — King Saud University

Where Curiosity Turned Into Direction

I entered King Saud University to study Computer Information Systems with a broad interest in technology, but it didn’t take long for cloud and infrastructure to stand out. Those years gave me more than technical foundations they shaped the way I think about systems, business needs, and how technology can solve real problems.
May 2017 — Al Tayyar Travel Group

First Exposure to Real Infrastructure

My internship at Al Tayyar gave me my first practical experience with enterprise networking. I worked on configuring switches and routers and learned the basics of network segmentation and security through VLANs and firewalls. It was a short experience, but it gave me an early appreciation for how critical strong infrastructure is behind every service.
Jul 2017 — Dell EMC Co-op

The Moment Cloud Became Serious for Me

My co-op at Dell EMC was a turning point. Working around enterprise storage platforms like VMAX, Unity, and VNX exposed me to the scale, architecture, and complexity of real cloud infrastructure. It was the first time I saw how the underlying layers of storage, compute, and design come together to power enterprise environments.
2017–2018 — KSU Graduation Project

Building Something People Could Actually Use

For my graduation project, I co-developed a Real Estate iOS app designed to improve the property search experience in Saudi Arabia. It was my first real end-to-end product experience taking an idea, shaping the user journey, and building something intended for real people rather than just academic evaluation.
Jul 2018 — IBM

Acceleration Through Advocacy, Platforms & Cloud

Joining IBM as a Developer Advocate gave me momentum early in my career. I delivered 35+ workshops to the IBM Cloud community, worked with private cloud environments using RHEL, Docker, and Kubernetes, and built chatbot solutions with Watson Assistant and Node.js. It was a fast, demanding chapter that sharpened both my communication skills and my technical confidence.
2019 — ElHaq

Building Around a Real Market Moment

When cinemas reopened in Saudi Arabia, I co-built ElHaq to help users track ticket releases the moment they became available. It was a timely product built around a real user frustration. That experience deepened my understanding of speed, relevance, and how satisfying it is to build something that solves a problem people genuinely feel.
Aug 2019 — solutions by stc

Moving Into the Commercial Side of Cloud

At solutions by stc, I stepped into business development and began working closer to cloud portfolios, partner ecosystems, and digital product strategy. I contributed to competitive analysis, managed relationships across the cloud marketplace, and supported portfolio growth through events, partnerships, and market-facing activities.

Expanding Into Portfolio Leadership

Sep 2024 — SITE

Where Business, Delivery & Technical Depth Converged

Joining SITE felt like the point where the different parts of my career finally came together. Product management had given me strong business grounding, but I wanted to be closer to technical shaping, customer conversations, and real delivery ownership. At SITE, I found that space. For the first time, I was able to connect both sides helping define the solution before the deal and leading the technical path after it. I’ve supported major cloud migrations, including the movement of 15,000 email users and 400+ virtual machines, while also driving proof of concepts around LLMs, RAG, and agentic AI. This role reflects how I work best: close to customers, close to delivery, and close to the technology itself.

Personal Projects

iOS AppIBM Cloud2019

ElHaq — تطبيق الحق

When Saudi Arabia opened its cinemas in 2019, getting a good seat was nearly impossible. Tickets would sell out within minutes, and there was no simple way to track when new seats were released. I built ElHaq to solve that. The app monitored ticketing websites and alerted users the moment tickets dropped, so they no longer had to keep refreshing pages manually. I built the iOS experience while the backend ran on IBM Cloud.
What I Learned
This project taught me what it feels like to build under real demand pressure. It pushed me to move fast, keep the backend lean, and focus on solving a problem people genuinely cared about. More than anything, it showed me how rewarding it is when something you build becomes immediately useful.
Web AppDirectory2020

The Coffee Stores

I built and launched thecoffeestores.com as a discovery platform for coffee shops, specialty beans, and roasters in Saudi Arabia. The goal was simple: make it easier for people to find the right places, products, and brands without the usual guesswork. It was a focused product built for a niche but passionate community.
What I Learned
This project taught me how to take an idea from zero to a live product on my own from building the site to thinking through how users would actually discover and use it. It also reinforced something important: when you build for a real community with clear needs, even a simple product can create strong value.
