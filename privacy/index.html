Privacy

This page explains the limited information that may be processed when you visit oalfawzan.sa.

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand general site usage, such as page visits, device types, approximate geographic region, and referral sources. Analytics information is used to improve the website and its content.

Cookies and Local Storage

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies. The photography gallery also uses local storage only to remember a randomized photo order in your browser. That gallery preference is not sent to the website owner.

External Links

The website links to third-party services including LinkedIn, Instagram, Snapchat, X, VSCO, Spotify, WhatsApp, and other external websites. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

Personal Information

The website does not provide user accounts, forms, a database, or an application backend for collecting visitor-submitted personal information.

Contact

For questions about this website or this privacy notice, use the contact information provided on the Experience page.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the website's analytics, hosting, or functionality changes. Last updated: 21 August 2026.
